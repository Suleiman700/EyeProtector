Built to protect, designed to disappear

No dashboards to babysit. EyeProtector does its job from the menu bar and only shows up when your eyes need it.

Short & long breaks

Frequent eye-rest pauses plus periodic get-up-and-stretch breaks, each on its own schedule you control.

Blink reminders

A two-second whisper of an overlay that reminds you to blink — because staring at screens cuts blinking in half.

Gentle or strict

Skippable breaks when you need flexibility, or locked-screen strict mode when you know you’ll cheat.

Smart idle skip

Away from your desk? Breaks are skipped and timers reset automatically. It never nags an empty chair.

Every display covered

Break overlays appear on all your monitors at once — no sneaking a glance at the second screen.

Set and forget

Launches at login, plays a soft chime if you want one, and stays out of your way the rest of the time.

Feels native, because it is

A settings window that reads like it shipped with your Mac. Everything is two clicks away.
